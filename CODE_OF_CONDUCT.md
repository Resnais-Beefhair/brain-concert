# Community guidelines

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, and publishing
someone's private information are not welcome in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive material and restrict participation.
For ordinary moderation concerns, use GitHub's report-content or report-abuse
controls rather than amplifying the material in an issue. Do not publish private
information in a report. Vulnerabilities have a separate [private channel](SECURITY.md).

These are lightweight project guidelines, not an adoption of a separate covenant
or a promise of a staffed enforcement team.
